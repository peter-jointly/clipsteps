🛒 Ingredients (family size — scale down for two)

  • Veg, chunky: his mix is leeks, onions, carrots, new potatoes, red capsicum — use whatever the box gave you this week (pumpkin, zucchini, parsnip, fennel all work)
  • Flavour: 1½ tsp sage (or fennel seeds), 4 cloves garlic, 2 tsp wholegrain mustard, salt, pepper, good glug of olive oil
  • On top: good butcher's sausages (chicken thighs work — see Step 7)
  • Finish: fresh parsley if you have it

🧰 Equipment

  • One big roasting tray and a frying pan for browning
