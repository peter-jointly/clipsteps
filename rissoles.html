🛒 Ingredients

  • Rissoles: good-quality beef mince (slightly fatty, not extra-lean), salt, pepper — our version adds 1 grated carrot + 1 grated zucchini
  • Onion gravy: 2 brown onions, olive oil + butter, ~1 tsp grated garlic, fresh thyme, Worcestershire sauce, ~1 tsp plain flour, beef stock
  • To serve: mashed potato and greens from the box (beans, broccoli, peas — whatever's in this week)
