🛒 Ingredients

  • Hidden veg: cauliflower (the star trick), butter, 1 tbsp miso paste, vegetable stock, half a head of garlic
  • Mornay: butter + flour (roux), milk, canned tuna, fresh corn (frozen or canned fine), chives, pasta spirals or penne
  • Cheese: mozzarella (stretch) + gruyère or cheddar (flavour) + parmesan (the crust)
  • Finish: lemon zest

🧰 Equipment

  • A pot for the cauliflower, a pot for pasta, and an oven-proof dish
  • Blender (or stick blender) for the silky puree
  • Grill (broiler) on high for the cheese crust
