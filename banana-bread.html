🛒 Ingredients

  • 3–4 ripe (or oven-rescued) bananas
  • Dry: 1¾ cups (215 g) plain flour · 1 tsp coarse salt (halve it if using fine salt) · ½ tsp cinnamon · 1 tsp bi-carb soda
  • Wet: ¾ cup (150 g) packed brown sugar · ½ cup (115 g) room-temperature unsalted butter · 2 room-temperature eggs · 1 tsp vanilla

🧰 Equipment

  • Two bowls, a whisk, a spatula, and a buttered loaf tin
  • Electric mixer helps for creaming, but hands work
