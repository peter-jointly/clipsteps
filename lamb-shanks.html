Lamb Shanks in Red Wine Sauce (+ Neck, Same Pot)

Guide built from RecipeTin Eats' video (3 min 49 s — tap the Overview to watch the whole cook) and Nagi's own written recipe, so every number below is hers. Her famous reassurance applies: “you literally cannot overcook lamb shanks.” Your pack's lamb neck runs the identical braise. Tap any step to jump the video there.

🛒 Ingredients (serves 4 — halve for the 2-pack)

  • 4 lamb shanks (~400 g each) · 1 tsp each salt and pepper · 2–3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion diced · 3 cloves garlic · 1 cup diced carrot · 1 cup diced celery
  • 2½ cups red wine (cheap and full-bodied is correct) · 800 g crushed tomatoes · 2 tbsp tomato paste · 2 cups low-salt chicken stock · 5 sprigs thyme (or 2 tsp dried) · 2 bay leaves
  • To serve: mash, polenta or cauliflower purée

🧰 Equipment

  • A big oven-proof pot with a lid · oven at 180 °C (slow cooker and pressure cooker options in Step 8)
