Ham & Cheese Scrolls — the Tuckshop Classic

Guide built from the Kidspot demo clip (1 min 11 s — tap the Overview to watch the whole thing) with exact quantities from Bake Play Smile's classic Australian recipe. No yeast, no rising — scone dough, roll, fill, slice, bake. Freeze the batch; a frozen scroll packed at 7am is defrosted by little lunch.

🛒 Ingredients (makes ~10 scrolls)

  • Dough: 300 g (2 cups) self-raising flour · 30 g chilled butter, chopped · ¾ cup milk (+ 2 tbsp extra for brushing)
  • Filling: ¼ cup mayonnaise · 150 g shaved ham · 80 g grated cheddar — our option: a layer of baby spinach or squeezed grated zucchini
  • Poppy seeds optional · swap mayo for tomato sauce, mustard or pesto; ham for other cold meats

🧰 Equipment

  • Bowl, rolling pin, and a lined 23 cm round tin (or a baking tray)
  • Oven at 200 °C fan (220 °C conventional)
