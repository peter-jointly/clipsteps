🧺 The formula (every box, every day)

  • 1 main — the star piece, usually from Sunday's bake
  • 1 fruit — whatever the box brought, cut so small hands can eat it
  • 1 crunch veg — sticks, rounds or little tomatoes
  • 1 extra — cheese, yoghurt, crackers, popcorn… Friday's extra is the treat
  • Water bottle always · no nuts (school rule) · ice brick in warm weather
