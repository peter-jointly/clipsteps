🛒 Ingredients

  • The dal: 250 g red lentils (washed), 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp ground cumin, 400 ml tin coconut milk + the tin refilled with water (+ ~300 ml more later)
  • The topping (tadka): 1 leek + 1 smallish onion thinly sliced, 2 tbsp oil + 1 tsp ghee (or butter), pinch of salt, 1 tbsp chilli oil
  • Rice to serve — or eat it straight as soup

🧰 Equipment

  • One pot for the dal, one frying pan for the leeks — done
