🛒 Ingredients

  • Chicken: drumsticks, skin on
  • Marinade: a slick of olive oil + 1 tbsp sesame oil, 5 tbsp honey, ¼ cup soy sauce, 3 cloves garlic, ½ tsp ground ginger (or 1 tsp fresh), cracked pepper
  • Finish: a knob of butter
  • Our tray: box roast veg — potato, pumpkin, carrot, onion wedges — plus greens to serve

🧰 Equipment

  • An oven-proof baking dish with a lid (or plastic wrap for the fridge)
  • A second oven tray for the veg
