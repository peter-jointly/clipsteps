Bangers & Mash with Real Onion Gravy

🛒 Ingredients (serves 2 — scales easily)

  • Good sausages — 95%+ meat, from the butcher if you can; ~3 per adult, 1–2 per child
  • Onion gravy: 2 medium brown onions, 1–1½ tsp sugar, 1 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp plain flour, rich beef stock, couple of sprigs of thyme, Worcestershire sauce
  • Mash: ~450 g potatoes, 2 tbsp butter, splash of milk, white pepper, salt
  • Greens: peas — or whatever green is in the box

🧰 Equipment

  • One wide pan (sausages, then the gravy in the same pan — the browned bits are flavour)
  • Pot for potatoes; potato ricer or masher
