Creamy Garlic Prawns — the Date-Night Classic

Guide built from RecipeTin Eats' video (1 min 41 s — tap the Overview to watch the whole cook) and Nagi's own written recipe, so every number below is hers. The Aussie date-night classic: 12 minutes of cooking, restaurant prices not included. Our zucchini-ribbon step slips the veg in.
🥕 1½ veg (with the ribbons)🍗 1 proteindate-night for two
0 of 9 done

🛒 Ingredients

  • Prawns: 500 g peeled prawns (large) · 1 clove garlic, minced · 1 tbsp olive oil · ½ tsp pepper · ½ tsp salt (added late) · 1 tbsp butter for cooking
  • Sauce: 2 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tbsp butter · ¼ cup dry white wine · ½ cup chicken or veg stock (low salt) · 1 cup cream · ½ cup finely grated parmesan · 2 tbsp parsley
  • Our veg: 1–2 zucchini in ribbons, or two big handfuls of spinach · mash, rice or bread to serve

🧰 Equipment

  • One large frying pan · a vegetable peeler for the zucchini ribbons
