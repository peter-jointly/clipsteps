🛒 Ingredients

  • Schnitzel: chicken breast (butterflied & flattened), plain flour, 3 eggs + splash of milk, panko breadcrumbs, neutral oil for shallow-frying, sea salt flakes
  • Mash: potatoes, unsalted butter (room temp), ~80 ml cream or milk, salt, white pepper
  • Diane sauce: butter, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp tomato paste, ~2 cups beef gravy, 1 tbsp Worcestershire, 80 ml (⅓ cup) thickened cream
  • Our salad: whatever's crisp in the box — leaves, cucumber, tomato, carrot ribbons

🧰 Equipment

  • Large pot for potatoes, saucepan for the sauce, and a high-rimmed pan or pot for frying
  • Meat mallet (or rolling pin), masher or ricer
  • A cooking thermometer helps — the oil wants to be 170 °C
